Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I log in?

Go to the Login page and enter your Student ID and Password.

2. How can I view my profile?

After logging in, click on the Profile option from the Dashboard.

3. How do I check my timetable?

Open the Dashboard and click on the Timetable option.

4. How can I reset my password?

Click on the Reset Password option from the Dashboard.

5. How do I contact the college?

Open the Contact Us page and use the contact details provided.
